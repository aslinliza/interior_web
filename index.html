BUDGETING

How Much Does a Modular Kitchen Really Cost in 2026?

By Radhika Krishnan · July 18, 2026 · 6 min read

If you've started collecting quotes for a modular kitchen, you've probably noticed the numbers vary wildly — anywhere from ₹1.5 lakh to ₹15 lakh for what sounds like the same request. The real answer depends on four things: carcass material, finish type, hardware brand, and layout complexity.

Carcass Material: The Foundation Cost

Marine-grade plywood costs more upfront than MDF or particle board, but it resists Indian humidity far better over a 10-year span. We consider this the single highest-leverage decision in your budget — it's the one component you genuinely cannot see or fix later.

"The cheapest kitchen is rarely the cheapest kitchen five years in."

Finish type is the second lever. Laminate remains the most budget-friendly option with excellent durability, while acrylic and PU finishes add a premium high-gloss look at roughly 20–35% more cost per running foot.

Curious what your kitchen would cost?

Explore our Modular Kitchen service page for real pricing tiers.

Explore Modular Kitchens

Hardware: Where Quality Quietly Shows Up

Soft-close hinges and telescopic channels rarely get discussed in a sales pitch, but they're what determines whether your kitchen still feels new in year seven. We'd always recommend allocating slightly more budget here over cosmetic upgrades elsewhere.

A Realistic 2026 Range

For a standard 10x10 ft Indian kitchen with mid-range laminate finish and branded hardware, expect somewhere between ₹2.8L and ₹4.5L all-inclusive. Larger island layouts or full acrylic finishes can push this toward ₹6L–₹8L.
